📱 ThorPad 2.0: put any app on the bottom screen, with a floating button to get the trackpad back.

## ✨ What's new in 2.0
- 📱 **Any app on the bottom screen:** a new 📱 **Apps** button in the top menu opens an app picker. Pick an app and it opens on the bottom screen, while your game keeps running on top
- 🗂️ **App picker:** your pinned apps first, then every app, with search on ThorPad's own keyboard. Tap an app to select it and again to open it, or use the big **Open here** button. Hold an app to pin or unpin it
- 🖱️ **Floating ThorPad button:** a small round button floats over the app:
  - 👆 **Tap** it to bring the trackpad back (the app keeps running)
  - ✋ **Hold** it for a quick menu: Trackpad, Keyboard, Switch app, Close app
  - 🤏 **Drag** it anywhere and it snaps to the nearest edge, and it fades after 3 seconds so it doesn't cover the app
- ↩️ **Back to your app:** with an app open down there, a "Back to" button always sits in the trackpad's bottom-left corner, so one tap takes you back to it, even after using the trackpad
- ⚙️ **Settings > Apps:** button size, fade, snap to edges, a swipe-in-from-the-edge option instead of the button, the "Back to" button, and your pinned apps
- ⌨️ Text boxes in the app still get the docked ThorPad keyboard

## 🖱️ What ThorPad does
ThorPad turns the AYN Thor's bottom screen into a trackpad and full keyboard for the top screen.
- 🖱️ **Real mouse:** with Shizuku, Android and every app see a real USB mouse with a scroll wheel, so GameNative and Winlator games work; touch mode works without it
- 🧈 **Smooth scrolling:** optional small-step scrolling that glides after a flick, with adjustable scroll speed
- 👆 **Gestures:** tap to click, hold or two-finger tap to right click, tap-and-slide to drag, two fingers to scroll or go back and forward, three fingers for recent apps, closing the top app, or a tap to close them all
- 👇 **Out of the way:** the whole screen is trackpad, with the menu a swipe down from the top
- ⌨️ **Full-screen keyboard:** big edge-to-edge keys (or a half-screen keyboard with the trackpad still usable above it, or docked under a text box in an app on the bottom screen), hold ⌫ for **Clear all**, and it opens and closes with text boxes when ThorPad is your Android keyboard, even when ThorPad itself is closed
- 🌐 **Six languages:** English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, with accents on a long press
- ✨ **Smart typing:** autocorrect that splits run-together words, adds accents and reads context (English), double-space period and auto-capitals
- 🖥️ **PC mode:** PC keys (Esc, F1 to F12, Windows shortcuts, sticky Ctrl / Alt / Shift) and PC gestures that switch on by themselves when Moonlight, a Moonlight fork, Steam Link, Parsec or a remote desktop app is on top
- 📳 **Haptics:** pick a feel and strength for every action
- 🔋 **Easy on the battery:** dims the trackpad when idle and runs nothing in the background
- 🔄 **Updates:** check, see what's new since your version, and install from Settings, or reinstall the current release
- 🗑️ **Clean uninstall:** one button undoes every change ThorPad made and removes it
- 📱 **Apps mode:** run any app on the bottom screen, with a floating button that brings the trackpad back

## 📲 Install
New to ThorPad? Download `ThorPad.apk` below, open it on the Thor to install, then open ThorPad and follow the setup checklist.

Already have it? In ThorPad, open Settings > **Check for updates**, or download `ThorPad.apk` below and open it on the Thor to install over the old version. Your settings are kept.
